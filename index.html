About ExamPro DSU Student Portal

ExamPro DSU is an independent student portal and exam management web application. It is designed to provide students with comprehensive academic services including student registration, login, hall ticket download, examination schedules, results, internal marks, attendance, fee payment, course registration, and access to an academic dashboard. ExamPro DSU is not the official portal of Dayananda Sagar University (DSU); it is an independent academic project.

Key Features of ExamPro DSU

Student Registration

New students can complete online student registration on ExamPro DSU by providing their personal information, academic details, and uploading required documents. The student registration system enables quick and secure enrollment into the portal.

Student Login

Registered students can securely log in to the ExamPro DSU student portal using their credentials. The student login system uses Firebase authentication for secure access to academic data.

Hall Ticket Download

Students can download their examination hall ticket directly from the ExamPro DSU portal. The hall ticket contains important examination details including exam schedule, exam center, seating information, and student identification.

Examination Results

ExamPro DSU provides a dedicated results section where students can view their semester examination results, subject-wise scores, and overall performance analytics.

Internal Marks

Students can check their internal marks and continuous assessment scores through the ExamPro DSU portal. The internal marks section displays subject-wise evaluation data and component-level breakdowns.

Attendance Tracking

The ExamPro DSU attendance module helps students track their attendance percentage across all subjects. Students can view attendance records and check eligibility for examinations based on attendance criteria.

Fee Payment

ExamPro DSU provides an academic fee payment module where students can view fee details, payment history, and manage their academic fee transactions securely.

Semester Exams

ExamPro DSU manages semester examination workflows including exam schedule announcements, hall ticket generation, result publication, and grade management for university semester exams.

Course Registration

Students can register for courses and subjects through the ExamPro DSU portal. The course registration system allows students to select electives and manage their academic curriculum.

Academic Dashboard

The ExamPro DSU academic dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of a student's academic performance, attendance, pending fees, upcoming exams, and recent results — all in one centralized student portal view.

University ERP System

ExamPro DSU functions as an independent university ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system for student management. It integrates academic records, examination management, attendance, internal marks, and campus services into a unified platform.

Campus Management

ExamPro DSU supports campus management functions by digitizing academic processes including student information management, examination administration, results processing, and academic reporting for campus stakeholders.
